# NO AMOUNT OF GOOD v2 rough

Watched that Carmy scene twice last night. Second time at like 1:40am with the sound low so I would not wake anyone. He says it without acting it. I do not need to provide any amusement or enjoyment. I do not need to receive any amusement or enjoyment. I am completely fine with that because no amount of good is worth how terrible this feels.

I sat there a while after. Because I have heard that sentence before, just not on TV.

It was in my own kitchen in Chicago in February when it gets dark at 4 and stays dark. It was in texts I never sent. It was in that stretch after I got it wrong in public and got cut in private and decided the safest plan was to want very little for a while. Work. Gym. Sleep. Scroll InKind for a place I will not book. Repeat.

Look. I get the math.

After the big one, and yours might be a breakup or a betrayal or money or family stuff where you were the problem, you start doing this quiet accounting. A laugh costs three days of replaying the night in the car. A date costs two weeks of checking your phone like an idiot. A new friend costs another story you might have to carry if it goes bad. Even small fun starts feeling expensive. New spot on Devon. Group chat that moves too fast. Song you used to love with someone you do not talk to now.

So you narrow. Same wake time because why not. Same coffee, same cup, same chair. Phone face down at dinner even when you are eating alone, which is stupid and I still do it. You tell people you are locked in. Some of that is true. A lot of it is you being scared to feel light for an hour and then pay for it for a month. I know because I lived that version at 24. Told myself discipline. It was mostly fear with a better haircut.

Pleasure needs surprise to work. That part took me too long to get. Food has to catch you off guard. Music has to find the nerve you did not plan to show. Another person has to have space to disappoint you or it never feels real when they show up right. If the worst thing in your life came from something unpredictable, and for most of us it did, your body starts filing all unpredictability in the same drawer. Joy sits right next to ambush. You open the drawer a crack and both jump out.

My Baa never bought the pep talk stuff. She could tell when someone was performing care. I think about that a lot with these guys who go quiet. They can smell a performance from a mile away now. Which makes normal help backfire completely.

Because the standard rescue is kind of insulting if you are in it. Get out more. Healing is linear. Brother you just need routine, ice bath, 5am club, sunlight. I swear people mean well. My inbox has a few of those texts saved. No pressure, just checking in, which somehow still needs a warm reply with exclamation points or you feel guilty for being the sad friend. Then the surprise visit where your place is not ready and you have to perform okayness in sweatpants. Then the advice that starts with you just need to and ends with something that worked for a person who never sat where you sit.

It adds bills. You were already broke. Now you owe energy to the people trying to save you.

I am not going to pretend I cracked this. I have not. I do not know the way out in any clean sense and anyone selling five steps has not lived it.

Two small things I actually saw work, both slow and kind of ugly, no montage.

One guy I know kept everything the same except Friday. Same run on Western, same time, except Friday he left the podcast at home and took whatever street looked quieter. Hated it. Told me it felt pointless and itchy for like three weeks straight. Week four some little dog followed him for two blocks near the park and he laughed before he could catch it. He said the laugh freaked him out more than it helped. That stuck with me. The first good feeling scared him. Still. A crack is a crack.

Other one quit explaining himself to death. He used to send these long paragraphs about why he could not make dinner, childhood wound plus work stress plus need recharge, whole essay. Nobody asked for the essay. Now he sends like four words. Not tonight. Still love you. Some friends fell off. Honestly good. The ones who stayed stopped needing a show. He stopped mixing up audience with friends, which honestly I am still learning.

Neither got ambushed into joy or whatever. They just paid a slightly smaller toll.

Carmy is right about the weight. Terrible does feel heavier. Anyone who paid that tax with their own name on it knows it in the chest, not as an idea. And walking away from everything good can feel like control when control is the only thing you have left. Keeps the kitchen running. Keeps you sober another day. Keeps you from texting the person at midnight.

The only part I would push back on, gently, because I have said his line myself with a straight face, is fine. I am completely fine with that. He is not fine. He is armored up. Armor works. Winter in Chicago, you need the heavy coat. You can live in it January through March. Problem starts when it is June and you are still zipped to the chin at a barbecue sweating through it and calling it preference.

No amount of good is worth how terrible this feels. I signed a shorter version of that contract. Maybe you did too. I get why.

I am just starting to think the renewal costs more than the protection. Not in a big speech way. In a small Tuesday way. One street different. One song all the way through without skipping at the bridge because it reminds you of her. One meal where the phone stays in your pocket the whole time even though your thumb gets itchy around minute six. Mine did last night.

You do not need amusement as a lifestyle. Nobody does. You might need tiny proof that surprise still holds something besides punishment.

I am still on that street myself,tbh. Some days I take the same road because I am tired. Some Fridays I leave the corner open.
