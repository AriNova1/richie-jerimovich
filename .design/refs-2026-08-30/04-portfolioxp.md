# pauljaguin.com — PortfolioXP (Awwwards Honorable Mention, Apr 2025)

Previously I judged this from the Awwwards thumbnail and wrote it off as "nostalgia,
honourable mention not SOTD, therefore cute not world-class." Having actually walked it,
that was wrong on the part that matters. The nostalgia is the skin. The structure
underneath is the best argument in this whole reference set for why an OS metaphor is
not a gimmick.

## The entry sequence is three gates deep

1. **Boot screen.** Black, the XP logo, the real animated segmented progress bar,
   "Bienvenue sur mon ordinateur."
2. **Login screen.** Blue gradient, a user tile with an avatar, "Pour commencer, cliquez
   sur mon nom d'utilisateur." A language selector sits bottom-left.
3. **Desktop.** Bliss wallpaper, icons, taskbar with a working Start button and a clock.

Three screens before any content. Most sites would call that friction. It is the whole
reason the fiction holds: by the time you reach the desktop you have already agreed to
be somewhere.

## Details I completely failed to consider

- **Custom cursors as real `.cur` files.** `cursor: url("/img/cursors/default-cursor.cur")`
  and `pointer-cursor.cur`. Not a CSS shape, not an SVG — actual Windows cursor binaries.
  Cheap, and it changes the feel of every single pixel of pointer movement.
- **Sound.** `start-windows.mp3` loads and plays on boot. The startup chime. I did not
  consider audio at all in any of my three concepts.
- **The document title tracks the active surface.** It changes to
  `Bureau | Paul Jaguin` on the desktop and updates per window. The browser tab is
  treated as part of the OS chrome.
- **Toasts carry real content.** Two appeared unprompted: one offering fullscreen "pour
  une expérience plus immersive", one saying hello with GitHub and buy-me-a-coffee
  buttons. The about/contact surface is a notification, not a page.
- Tahoma throughout, the actual XP UI face. 43 image assets for icons.

## The IA does real work

Opening "Mes projets" gives a full Explorer window: the XP left rail (Tâches système /
Autres / Détails), a toolbar with Back, Search and Folders, an address bar, and the
projects as file icons grouped under **Sites web** and **Serveurs**.

Projects are files. Categories are folders. Contact is a notification. A CV is a
document. The metaphor is not decoration over an ordinary site — it is a filing system,
and a portfolio genuinely is a filing problem.

`scrolls: false` again. Fourth reference, fourth fixed viewport.

## What I take from this

- **Earn the entry.** A boot and a login are not friction if the thing you are entering
  is worth entering. VIGIL currently drops you straight onto a finished desk.
- **Sound is available and I ignored it.** One chime at the moment the machine wakes.
- **Custom cursor is the cheapest immersion in the set.**
- Notifications are a legitimate content surface, not just chrome.
- The reason this only got an Honorable Mention is not the structure. It is that the
  skin is somebody else's. Take the structure, bring an original skin.
