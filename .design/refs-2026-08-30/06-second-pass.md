# Second pass — the screenshots I did not get to

Rick sent captures of states my browser pane never reached. The Silo ones overturn my
read of that site a second time.

## SILO is a full web desktop. I only ever saw its loading bar.

Three states I never reached:

**1. The phosphor desktop.** Near-black with a faint green **grid** ruled across the
whole field, small node dots at every intersection. Icons are **thin green wireframe
folders**, not filled: `SILO-EP301 … EP307`, `SILO-MISC`, `SILO-DRIVE`, `SILO-RELICS`,
and `SILO-NOTES` **with a padlock on the folder**. A concentric-circle "Spiral" icon.
Menubar: a running clock `08:41:30`, a search glyph, a speaker glyph, `SILO` set right.
This is the most beautiful thing in the whole reference set and it is exactly the
"under the hood" language.

**2. The photographic desktop.** Same OS, same icon grid — but the wallpaper is a
**night aerial of Washington DC**, Capitol and Washington Monument lit, long-exposure
traffic on the bridge, and the icons switch to ordinary filled blue folders. Apple TV
logo joins the menubar.

So the site ships **two skins over one desktop**: a wireframe/schematic mode and a
photographic mode. Same furniture, different truth. That is a mechanism, not decoration.

**3. "Silo View" — a six-up surveillance grid.** Six real video clips from the show
playing at once, each with its own mute toggle, over a scanline wash. Beneath it, two
panels: **Settings** (Input Latency 100ms · Memory 2048MB · Units Metric · Workgroup
SES-008 · Security 256-bit · AdvSec Enabled · Firewall Enabled · IP Status Static ·
Time Zone +2.00 · Refresh Rate 100 · Sensors Buffer 1024) and **Monitor**, a live
area chart. Fake system telemetry as content.

For agentrichie this is almost too on the nose: **41 recorded nights** want a six-up
grid, and the Settings panel wants Richie's *real* vitals instead of invented ones.

## Techniques worth stealing from the rest

- **holidayOS (tonik)** — a **dithered halftone photograph** as the wallpaper, sepia,
  1-bit. It lets a real photo live inside a limited-palette OS without breaking it.
  Menubar carries a live counter: "We planted: 920 trees". Stats rendered as three
  icon+number cells inside a window.
- **heaven.computer** — soap **bubbles drifting** across a sky wallpaper, continuously.
  Ambient particles are the cheapest aliveness there is. Icon names do the work:
  God Observer, God Remixer, Mall Of Tortured Souls, Sky Luxury.
- **robbyyeager.com** — a 1996 desktop that **states its own concept inside the fiction**:
  "Yes, it's a 1996 desktop, art-directed in 2026. A deliberate concept: taste, systems
  thinking, and interaction design, disguised as nostalgia." Cycling **"Did you know?"**
  toasts top-right, and a fake **"RobbyOS Update — a newer version of this portfolio is
  available"** notification whose Install button is the link to his modern site. The
  wink defuses "is this just nostalgia" before the visitor can ask it.
- **matthewpmunger** — pure 1-bit System 7. Striped title bars made of horizontal rules,
  chunky pixel display face, dithered scrollbar troughs, 1px black borders, no colour at
  all. Proof that a total graphic identity can come from black, white and line pattern.
- **clickbecause.net** — the minimal end: black title bars, coloured traffic lights,
  monospace body, file icons right-aligned. `lena.jpg` opens as a deliberately corrupted
  image. Restraint plus one joke.
- **PostHog changelog, full width** — four week columns side by side, each independently
  scrolling, avatars per entry, and the year scrubber beneath with density squares.

## What this does to the three concepts Rick picked

- **THE ZOOM** — the innermost depth now has a language: Silo's phosphor grid and
  wireframe icons. Outside is photographic, inside is schematic, and the transition
  between them is literally "going under the hood".
- **THE SIGNAL** — Silo View is the proof this works: a multi-up video wall of the 41
  recorded nights, scanlines, and a telemetry panel that reads the real vitals.
- **THE RIG** — holidayOS's dithered 1-bit halftone is the right register for a machine
  that does not exist, and matthewpmunger shows how far pure line pattern can carry it.

And one house rule from robbyyeager: **say the concept out loud inside the work.** The
current site's problem was pretending. A line that admits what this is would cost
nothing and buy a lot.
